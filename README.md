PlaySense 🎮🧠

PlaySense is an AI-powered gameplay co-pilot for open-world and PC games. It captures your current gameplay context through screenshots, optional OBS replay clips, voice input, and AI vision analysis to provide spoiler-controlled assistance in real time.

Designed as a lightweight desktop companion, PlaySense helps players decide what to do next, understand mechanics, improve tactics, and navigate complex games without constantly searching external guides or videos.

Safety & Design Philosophy

PlaySense is designed as a companion application rather than a cheat utility.

The application:

Does not inject into games
Does not modify memory
Does not alter save files
Uses screenshots, voice input, and OBS integration for contextual assistance
